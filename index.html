Selected research & engineering projects

2026 · Current

Battery Thermal Behaviour & Machine Learning Pipeline

Built an end-to-end workflow connecting a PyTorch model to MATLAB/Simulink thermal modelling. The project progressed through model training, Python-to-MATLAB integration, Simulink response testing, environmental scenarios and AI-controlled cooling. A closed-loop test produced a 9.03°C final temperature reduction, from 37.12°C without AI cooling to 28.09°C with AI cooling.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, MATLAB, Simulink, Simscape, Matplotlib.
